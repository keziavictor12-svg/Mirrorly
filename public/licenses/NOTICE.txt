MediaPipe canonical face measurements

public/tracking.js includes a 15-landmark numeric subset of the canonical
face model published by The MediaPipe Authors under Apache License 2.0.
The subset has been adapted for Mirrorly's pose-projected fitting algorithm.
tests/fixtures/mediapipe-canonical-points.json contains all 468 canonical
vertices from that same model for synthetic contour/occlusion regressions.
These are generic model coordinates, not captured customer face data.
Source: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/v0.10.21/mediapipe/modules/face_geometry/data/canonical_face_model.obj
